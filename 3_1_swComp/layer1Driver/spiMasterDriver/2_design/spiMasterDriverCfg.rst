spiMasterDriverCfg.h
########################

Defines compile-time feature flags for the SPI Master Driver:

- ``BPB_SPI_MASTER_CFG_ENABLE_DMA``: Enables DMA-based SPI transfers.
- ``BPB_SPI_MASTER_CFG_ENABLE_CALLBACKS``: Enables transfer-complete callback registration.
