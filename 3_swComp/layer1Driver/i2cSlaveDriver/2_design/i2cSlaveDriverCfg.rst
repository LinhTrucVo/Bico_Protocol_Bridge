i2cSlaveDriverCfg.h
########################

Defines compile-time feature flags for the I2C Slave Driver:

- ``I2C_SLAVE_CFG_ENABLE_DMA``: Enables DMA-based I2C transfers.
- ``I2C_SLAVE_CFG_ENABLE_CALLBACKS``: Enables RX, TX, and address-match callback registration.
